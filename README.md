# Ornithopter for Surveillance

Published research paper focused on biomimicry-inspired ornithopter technology for aerial surveillance and reconnaissance applications.

---

## Abstract

This research paper explores the advancement of ornithopter technology by combining biomimicry, stealth operations, and surveillance systems. Inspired by the flight mechanisms of birds and insects, the project studies how flapping-wing UAVs can be used for covert surveillance missions with improved maneuverability, silent operation, and environmental adaptability.

The paper also discusses the integration of ESP32 CAM modules, wireless communication systems, and flight control components to enhance surveillance capabilities.

---

## Key Features

- Biomimicry-based flight mechanism
- Stealth surveillance concepts
- Flapping-wing UAV architecture
- ESP32 CAM integration for wireless monitoring
- Remote reconnaissance capabilities
- Lightweight and agile aerial system design
- Enhanced maneuverability for urban environments

## Paper Overview

The paper focuses on the future potential of ornithopters in:
- Military surveillance
- Covert reconnaissance
- Environmental monitoring
- Autonomous aerial robotics

The study highlights how biomimetic UAV systems can provide advantages over traditional drones in terms of stealth, flexibility, and adaptability.

---

## Conclusion

This research demonstrates the growing potential of ornithopter technology in modern aerial surveillance systems. By combining natural flight inspiration with modern electronics and wireless communication technologies, ornithopters can become an innovative solution for next-generation reconnaissance and intelligent monitoring systems.
